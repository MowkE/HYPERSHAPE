Controls

Ssave a PNG screenshot

Reading the picture: every point of a 4D shape has coordinates (x, y, z, w). The first three are drawn in space; the fourth is drawn as color (see the legend). The three 4D rotation planes (XW, YW, ZW) trade spatial position for w — you see geometry flow through the color spectrum instead of turning.
